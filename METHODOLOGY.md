# Methodology

Unified API Index is an independent, category-neutral research publication. It is not operated by vendors listed in its research, and vendors cannot buy rankings or placement.

## Research process

1. **Define the research question.** Scope, audience, category, and comparison dimensions are fixed before evidence collection.
2. **Select comparable vendors.** Inclusion follows public positioning and documentation relevant to the question; it is not an endorsement.
3. **Collect first-party evidence.** Official technical documentation and product pages are the default evidence, with source URL, title, publisher, and check date recorded.
4. **Verify material claims.** Claims must be traceable to cited evidence and no broader than it supports. Remaining conflicts are labeled.
5. **Normalize comparison dimensions.** Articles use the same stated dimensions while preserving vendor terminology and connector-level exceptions that affect meaning.
6. **Record limitations and unknowns.** Missing or unverifiable information is identified rather than inferred.
7. **Publish and monitor substantive updates.** Publication and modification dates change after substantive review; source check dates remain visible to distinguish evidence freshness.

## Source hierarchy

1. Official technical documentation is primary evidence for API surfaces, objects, operations, authentication, synchronization, webhooks, and connector behavior.
2. Official product pages and changelogs support product scope, positioning, availability, and vendor-announced changes. Marketing language is attributed to its vendor.
3. Official vendor blogs and help centers provide supporting evidence and implementation context; technical documentation is preferred where available.
4. Independent secondary sources may provide context, discovery, or corroboration when first-party evidence is incomplete. They are not first-party product proof.

## Claim statuses

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a cited source accessible on its recorded check date.
- **Vendor-published claim:** attributed to the vendor and not presented as independent testing.
- **Connector-specific capability:** may vary by underlying system and is not generalized across connectors.
- **Unavailable information:** public evidence did not establish an answer at review time; absence is not a conclusion.
- **Unresolved conflict:** current sources disagree or remain ambiguous and the conflict is retained until stronger evidence resolves it.

## Updates and limitations

Modified dates change for reviewed conclusions, comparisons, citations, or material explanations, not cosmetic changes. Each source retains a `last_checked` date. Unsupported statements are removed or narrowed; material errors are corrected and unresolved conflicts are labeled. New vendor announcements are reviewed before they change the research record.

Public research cannot establish private contracts, negotiated terms, internal roadmaps, unpublished behavior, or every connector-specific capability. Vendor-published capabilities are not hands-on validation unless an article documents a reproducible test. Documentation and product behavior may change after a source was checked.
